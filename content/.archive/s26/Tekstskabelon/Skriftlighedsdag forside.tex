\subsection{Assignment – Short Story (Fiction)}
Write a short horror story (600-800 words) that includes the following elements:

\begin{itemize}
\item Something \qm{uncanny}.
\item Foreshadowing.
\item A character whose personality is both shown (indirect characterisation) and told (direct characterisation).
\end{itemize}

Additional guidelines:
\begin{itemize}
\item Think about the tone of your short story, and try to use descriptive language to match that tone.
\item Try to add unique elements to your story that will make it stand out from other horror stories.
\item Think about whether your story is an exploration of one overall theme, and try to include lines that make this theme as clear as possible.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Assignment – Short Story (Fiction)}
Write an analytical essay on Haruki Murakami’s short story \qm{On Seeing the 100\% Perfect Girl One Beautiful April Morning}.

Part of your essay must focus on the effect of the short story's preoccupation with dream and reality. It should also include:
\begin{itemize}
\item A \textit{characterisation} of the main character.
\item An intepretation of the short story's \textit{theme} and \textit{message}.
\end{itemize}

Your essay must include references to the short story.
