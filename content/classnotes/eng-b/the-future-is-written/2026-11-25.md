## On a Sunbeam

> [!task] Read
> 1. Read the first two (or three) chapters of the webcomic [On a Sunbeam](https://www.onasunbeam.com/)
>     - Pay close attention to the shifting backgrounds.
>     - Note the facial expressions and body language of the characters.

![1795014338-sunbeam.png](imgs/1795014338-sunbeam.png "right")

---

> [!task] Discuss
> 1. Why did Mia sign up for the job? What did she expect and what did she get?
> 2. What do we learn about the society that used to exist where Mia and her crew do construction work?
> 3. What do we learn about Mia’s job?
> 4. Make a list of the names of the characters with a small description of who they are and how they are related to the other characters.

## Analysis

> [!task-columns] Work with the text
> > [!task] Option A
> > 1. Read your feedback for "Essay 2".
> > 2. Write a paragraph incorporating the feedback where you analyse one aspect of the graphic novel. Use [this guide](../../../classnotes/eng-b/notes/how-to-analyse.md) when choosing aspects, and [the ACE-model](../../../classnotes/eng-b/notes/ace-model.md) to write your paragraph.
> > 3. Write a paragraph explaining how you understood and addressed the feedback in your paragraph.
>
> > [!task] Option B
> > 1. Choose a chapter to add caption boxes to.
> > 2. Find at least 5 panels to write a caption box to. One of your panels must be a silent panel.
> > 3. Take screenshots of the panels and add the boxes to the panels using e.g. canva.
> > 4. Discuss with someone who also chose this option what and if your narrative voice adds anything significant to the story.
