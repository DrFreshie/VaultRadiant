## Born a crime – Analysis

Yesterday: questions for the first 5 pages (*OBS: read till the top of page 6: ending with "I was born a crime"*)
1. What genre is the text?
2. Comment on the title.
3. How does Noah explain the historical origins of apartheid? Why include this?
4. How does Noah blend personal anecdotes with historical explanation? What effect does this have on the reader?
5. Why does Trevor Noah talk about this subject in his comedy? Why write about it?
6. Pick one other text (this includes the photos) that we have worked with, and relate it to *Born a crime*.

Today: questions for the rest of the text:
1. How does Noah describe the experience of mixed-race children under apartheid?
2. How does his mother manipulate the system to protect him? What does this show about agency and resistance under oppressive laws?
3. How does Noah's first-person perspective shape your understanding of apartheid?
4. How does the childhood perspective influence the tone of the text?
