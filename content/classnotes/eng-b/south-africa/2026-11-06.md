## Work questions for the movie

1. As the film begins we view a pristine rugby pitch and across the street a rundown soccer field. Discuss the symbolism of these images.
2. What is the political situation at the beginning of the film?
3. How is Mandela portrayed throughout the movie?
4. What is Pienaar's attitude towards Mandela?
5. Mandela requests that the rugby team go out into the poorer communities to work with the youth. How did the team react at first? How did they change over time? What was the purpose of these events?
6. How does Mandela's security team evolve over the course of the film?
7. How does the audience react differently to Mandela's appearance at the stadium at the end of the film compared to his first appearance at the game at the beginning of the film?
8. Discuss how Nelson Mandela used the idea of "sport" to unify South Africa.
9. At one point Mandela states "Forgiveness liberates the soul. It removes fear. That is why it is such a powerful weapon". Discuss this statement.
10. The rugby team visits one of the prisons that Mandela was at for 27 years. Why do you think they took the team to the prison? Is there power in being in spaces of cultural and historical significance? Why or why not?
11. Pienaar asks the team to learn the words to the new national anthem but the team refuses. At the final match in the World Cup you see them singing the song with the crowd. How has the team evolved over the course of the film?
12. South Africa, against all odds, wins the Rugby World Cup, and Mandela thanks Pienaar saying "Thank you for what you have done for your country" to which Pienaar says "No, Mr. President. Thank you." Discuss how Mandela and Pienaar helped each other and helped South Africa.
