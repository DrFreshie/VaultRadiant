## Analysis of the first 5 pages

Questions for the first 5 pages (*OBS: read till the top of page 6: ending with "I was born a crime"*):

1. What genre is the text? Discuss first and then you can use the internet to look it up.
2. Comment on the title.
3. How does Noah explain the historical origins of apartheid? Why include this?
4. How does Noah blend personal anecdotes with historical explanation? What effect does this have on the reader? Include references to the text.
5. Why does Trevor Noah talk about this subject in his comedy? Why write about it?
6. Pick one other text (this includes the photos) that we have worked with, and relate it to *Born a crime*.
