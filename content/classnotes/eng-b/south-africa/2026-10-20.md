## Reading *Once upon a time*

- What do you expect based on the title and the theme?
- While reading, note down all the ways in which the story resembles a fairy tale.
- [Nadine Gordimer Once-Upon-a-Time 1989.pdf](https://files.edaptio.com/rRro6U8aQxKGERX2LLwx_Nadime+Gordimer+Once-Upon-a-Time+1989.pdf)

## Analysis

Individually or in self-chosen groups, start answering the work questions – don't rush through it, we will get some more time tomorrow.

1. List all the fairy tale elements in the story. What is the overall effect of these elements? That is, why do you think the author has included them?
2. Is the story a fairy tale? Why/why not?
3. Compare the suburb of the story to the rest of the country in which it is situated. Include textual evidence (quotes).
    - What groups of people live in the suburb(s) and outside?
    - What are their respective living conditions?
    - How do they interact and regard each other?
    - What are their respective relations with the authorities?
    - In what actual country do you think the story is set – and when? Why?
4. How is the suburb in the story typical of suburbs in general? For instance, consider the lifestyle and values of the suburbanites in the text.
5. What might the wall around the family's plot symbolize?
6. Discuss the ending – why did Gordimer choose this ending, and is it effective?
7. Does the story carry a message? If so, what is it?
