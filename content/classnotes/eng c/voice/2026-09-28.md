## Rhetorical tools

- "She speaks in a calm, friendly and respectful way."
- "She uses “we,” “us” and “our” to include everyone"
- "She gives examples of things like oil spills and nuclear accidents to show how countries affect each other."
- "She should speak with empathy and responsibility"
- "She should avoid racism, fear and division."

## Devices

- alliteˈration: the repetition of a consonant sound at the beginning of words which are close together
- aˈnaphora: the repetition of one or more words at the beginning of successive clauses
- anˈtithesis: the placing together of opposing words or ideas to show a contrast
- ˈassonance: the repetition of vowel sounds in words which are close together
- ˈmetaphor: an image described by a word or phrase with a concrete meaning but used figuratively
- personifiˈcation: language in which an inanimate object or concept is spoken of as if it had human life
- repetition
- ˈsimile: a figure of speech in which two unlike things are compared using ‘like’ or ‘as’
- the list of three: three things are listed immediately after each other.
- inclusive language: language that addresses or includes the audience, often using pronouns such as ‘we’, ‘us’, and ‘our’

## Modes of appeal

- pathos: an appeal to the audience’s emotions using emotional language, passion and a personal angle
- logos: an appeal to reason with arguments, evidence, facts and logical reasoning
- ethos: an appeal to the audience’s trust by sounding eloquent, or by establishing authority or expertise on the subject.

**Example** "Trump uses antithesis when he says that America rejects globalism and embraces patriotism. This device is used to make a pathos appeal to patriotic feelings, which targets American voters and citizens, who can mediate political change by supporting his nationalist policies and voting for leaders who put America first."

## Grammatik: Substantiver

### Regelmæssige

| Regel | Singularis | Pluralis |
|---|---|---|
| Substantiver, der i **singularis** ender på en hvislelyd (en s-agtig lyd), får tilføjet **-es** i **pluralis**. | *glass*<br>*dish*<br>*box* | *glasses*<br>*dishes*<br>*boxes* |
| Substantiver, der ender på **-y** efter en konsonant, får tilføjet **-ies** i **pluralis**. | *family*<br>*baby*<br>*sky* | *families*<br>*babies*<br>*skies* |
| Nogle substantiver, der ender på **-o**, tilføjer **-es** i **pluralis**. | *potato*<br>*hero*<br>*tomato* | *potatoes*<br>*heroes*<br>*tomatoes* |
| Nogle substantiver, der ender på **-f/-fe**, ændres til **-ves** i **pluralis**. | *knife*<br>*half*<br>*wife* | *knives*<br>*halves*<br>*wives* |

### Uregelmæssige

| Regel | Singularis | Pluralis |
|---|---|---|
| Ved nogle substantiver ændres vokalen. | *man*<br>*foot*<br>*mouse* | *men*<br>*feet*<br>*mice* |
| Nogle substantiver tager endelsen **-en**. | *child*<br>*ox* | *children*<br>*oxen* |
| Nogle substantiver har samme form i **singularis** og **pluralis**. | *sheep*<br>*deer*<br>*series* | *sheep*<br>*deer*<br>*series* |
| Nogle substantiver, der er låneord, beholder latinsk/græsk **pluralisendelse**. | *hypothesis*<br>*phenomenon*<br>*cactus* | *hypotheses*<br>*phenomena*<br>*cacti* |
