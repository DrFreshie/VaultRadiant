## Establishing meaning

- Read until line 17 and establish meaning in the text by deciding:
    1. Who is speaking?
    2. Who else is there?
    3. Where is the speaking/writing taking place?
    4. What is the subject of the writing
    4. Who is the audience?
    5. Why is he writing?
    6. What is your first impression of the writer?

## Reading

### Prediction

- From what you have read now, what do you think has happened to Joey and the place he lives in? Will he get the ham? Write down you prediction and share it with another student.

## Group work

- To what extent did your predictions come true?
- Write down notes on the other half of the questions
