## Discussion

 1. What do we learn about Edith’s reason for returning to the Finch house, and how does this change our understanding of the game’s story?

 2. How does the final vignette connect Edith’s pregnancy to the themes of family, memory, and inheritance?

 3. What does Edith seem to believe about the “Finch curse” by the end of the game? Does she accept it, reject it, or something in between?

 4. How does the ending change the role of the player? What does it mean that the story is ultimately being read by Edith’s child?

 5. After finishing the game, what do you think the title What Remains of Edith Finch means? What “remains” of Edith, her family, and their stories?

## Examples

1. **Remember**  
   What are the five questions in the questionnaire given to Helly when she wakes up at the table?

2. **Understand**  
   Explain what the severance procedure does to a person’s memory and identity.

3. **Apply**  
   If you were explaining Lumon’s severance system to someone who had never seen the show, what real-life workplace situation could you compare it to?

4. **Analyze**  
   How does the episode create a contrast between Mark’s “innie” life at work and his “outie” life outside Lumon?

5. **Evaluate**  
   Do you think Helly’s choice to undergo severance is understandable? 

6. **Create**  
   If you were a journalist tasked with writing an article about the procedure, and you had an interview with Helly, what would you ask her?
