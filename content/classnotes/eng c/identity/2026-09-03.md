## Overview of cinematic techniques

| Cinematic technique | Characteristics |
|---|---|
| **Framing** | **Close-up:** The character(s) are framed very closely.<br><br>**Long shot:** Focus is on setting.<br><br>**Medium shot:** Focus is on more than one character. |
| **Angles** | **Low angle:** The character in question seems powerful and big.<br><br>**High angle:** The character in question seems small and maybe weak.<br><br>**Eye level:** Audience at the same level as characters. |
| **Composition** | Foreground.<br><br>Middle ground.<br><br>Background. |
| **Sound** | **Diegetic sound:** Sound which is part of the film.<br><br>**Non-diegetic sound:** Sound which is not part of the film.<br><br>**Contrapuntal sound:** Sound in direct contrast to the images of the film. |
| **Lighting** | **Low key:** Difficult to see everything in the image.<br><br>**High key:** Able to see everything in the image. |
| **Colour** | Each colour has both a positive and negative symbolism. |
