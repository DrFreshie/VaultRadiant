## Warm-up discussion

- Identify a role model from your childhood that had an afect on your identity formation.
- Identify a role model that you currently admire.
- What are the qualities or traits you admire in this person? Are they qualities that you are trying to develop in yourself? Has this person had any efect on the formation of your identity?
