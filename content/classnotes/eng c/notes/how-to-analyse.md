# How to analyse

## Fiction

### Setting

1. Describe the physical setting.
2. Analyse the effect: Does the setting create a certain atmosphere? Does this influence the story / characters?

### Narrator

1. Determine the type of narrator.
2. Analyse the effect: How does the narrator influence what we know about the characters and setting?

### Characterisation

1. Describe the external appearance main character(s).
2. Analyse their inner life and role in the story: What do we know about their values, personality, and relationships with the other characters? Do they develope?

### Theme

1. Analyse the theme of the story: What does the story say about the theme? How is it visible in the story?
