## Summing up The Relive Box

> [!task] Categorise
> 1. Look at your notes on your assigned questions from last lesson: Put your questions into one of the categories on the whiteboard.
> 2. Write down the letter of your questions as well as keywords connected to that question under the category on the whiteboard.

## Working with the text: part 1

> [!task] Quick write
> 1. Write an entry for an encyclopedia like Wikipedia on Nepenthe’s method of memory removal (100-200 words). Use formal and objective language and include the following terms: self-informed, self-confidential, the company name Nepenthe, traces and restoration. You can mimic [this wiki site on ECT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electroconvulsive_therapy).
> Make sure to include the backstory and development of the scientific procedure.

## Working with the text: part 2

> [!task] In groups
> 1. Record a ten minute, non-scripted conversation about the text and listen to it afterwards, OR
> 2. Make a poster that visually presents the themes of the novel excerpt, and connects it to Brave New World and The Relive Box, and possibly one other text. It should show how the texts connect, not just that they connect, OR
> 3. Mimicking the questions from last lesson, come up with 5 questions about the text. Discuss and answer the questions, AND
> 4. Make a summary of your work to present to class

> [!task] Individually
> 1. Prepare an oral presentation of the text with at least 5 bullets. Rehearse the presentation (no reading aloud), and make sure you can talk for 6 minutes, OR
> 2. Make a poster that visually presents the themes of the novel excerpt, and connects it to Brave New World and The Relive Box, and possibly one other text. It should show how the texts connect, not just that they connect, OR,
> 3. Mimicking the questions from last lesson, come up with 5 questions about the text. Answer the questions, AND
> 3. Make a summary of your work to present to class
