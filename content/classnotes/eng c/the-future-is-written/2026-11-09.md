## Working with the text

> [!task] Create
> 1. In groups, select and analyse a scene from the film that you particularly liked, found important or were intrigued by. In your analysis, you must include a focus on filmic devices such as close-ups, perspective, sound effects or lighting. Moreover, connect the scene to the episode as a whole and give reasons why you picked this scene. Create a slideshow with stills from the film, that presents your analysis.

## Written assignment

> [!task] Write
> 1. Read the article "How we feel about robots that feel"
> 2. Write an e-mail from Martha to the company that produced ‘Ash’. Your letter must try to pinpoint what it is that makes a human-robot relationship like this creepy. Be as specific as possible. You can either argue that the company ought to put an end to production or that the company needs to advertise the robots differently. Use formal language (500 words).
