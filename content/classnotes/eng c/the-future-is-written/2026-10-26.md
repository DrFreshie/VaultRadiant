## Memory

> [!task] vocabulary
> 1. The words and expressions below are all related to memory. Look up the ones you are not familiar with.
>
> *recollect – call to mind – a trip down memory lane – fix in the mind – hold dear – dwell on the past – recall – relive – think back – in retrospect – reminiscence – treasure – cherish – have a flashback – ring a bell – anamnesis – dig into the past – backtrace*

---

> [!task] In pairs
> 2. How many of the words and expressions from previous slide can you remember?
> 3. If you had the chance to relive a memory from your past, what would it be and why? Describe the memory, what happens, the mood, the setting etc. You may include some of the words from the list in the previous slide.

%% Sum up: In class, discuss to what extent you chose the same kinds of memories. What characterised your memories. Is there a common denominator? What is it that your classmates seem to be looking for? %%

## Formal vs. informal language

> [!task] In pairs or individually
> 1. Using your notes from your reading focus, write a short but instructive manual for the second-generation Halcom XI520 Relive Box (100-200 words). Use formal language.
> 2. Now write an unofficial manual for the internet using informal language (50-100 words). It is a manual for people who found it hard to understand the official manual that you wrote in the previous exercise.
