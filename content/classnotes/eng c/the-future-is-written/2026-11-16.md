> 2. Do you think humans will colonise Mars in your lifetime? How does this make you feel?

## Settled In

> [!task] Read
> 1. Read the short story Settled In by Tim Dudenhoefer

## Comprehension

> [!task] Discuss
> 1. Where are we when the story begins, and who are the people there?
> 2. Where are they headed?
> 3. Who and what are Percy and Jo?
> 4. What is Shackleton?
> 5. Who was the narrator’s father and what happened to him?
> 6. What has happened in the narrator’s personal life since leaving Earth?
> 7. What role did A.I. play in the human mission to Mars?

## Analysis

> [!task] Write
> 1. Choose 5 questions on the hand out. Answer each question in writing (at least 50 words).
> 2. Collect your answers into one text of 5 paragraphs. 
> 3. Try to make the text as cohesive as possible by adding [linking words](https://theenglishhandbook.systime.dk/?id=247#c1764), an introduction, and/or a conclusion.
