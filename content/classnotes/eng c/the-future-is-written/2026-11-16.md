## Settled In

> [!no-slide]
> Tim Dudenhoefer is a systems engineer working for NASA at the NASA Goddard Space Flight Center. He grew up outside of Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania along the shores of the Allegheny River where he learned to love the outdoors, reading, writing, science fiction and engineering. He has been writing stories since he was a child.
> 
> In 2026 NASA invited writers of sci-fi to submit stories about future journeys and the possible terraforming of Mars to a competition. NASA turned their attention to science fiction as they believed that it might help them “look beyond predictable outcomes and identify mitigation approaches for previously unforeseen risks.“ Tim Dudenhoefer’s story is one of the winning entries in the competition.

> [!task] Read
> 1. Read the short story Settled In by Tim Dudenhoefer

## Comprehension

> [!task] Discuss
> 1. Where are we when the story begins, and who are the people there?
> 2. Where are they headed?
> 3. Who and what are Percy and Jo?
> 4. What is Shackleton?
> 5. Who was the narrator’s father and what happened to him?
> 6. What has happened in the narrator’s personal life since leaving Earth?
> 7. What role did A.I. play in the human mission to Mars?

## Analysis

> [!task] Write
> 1. Choose 5 questions on the hand out. Answer each question in writing (at least 50 words).
> 2. Collect your answers into one text of 5 paragraphs. 
> 3. Try to make the text as cohesive as possible by adding [linking words](https://theenglishhandbook.systime.dk/?id=247#c1764), an introduction, and/or a conclusion.
