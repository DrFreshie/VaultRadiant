## Comprehension questions

1. What is Mr. Thomas Shap’s job?
2. How does he feel about his ordinary life and work?
3. What does he begin to imagine during his daily routine?
4. What is the name of the city he creates in his fantasy world?
5. Why does Mr. Shap decide that he is the king of Larkar?
6. How does his imagined world grow over time?
7. How does his fantasy life begin to affect his real life?
8. What important mistake does he make because of his dreams?
9. Why do other people begin to worry about him?
10. What does Mr. Shap plan at the end of his fantasy world?
11. Where is he really when the coronation takes place?
12. Who are the “warders,” and what do they tell him to do?

## Interpretation questions

1. Why does Mr. Shap create Larkar?
3. Can fantasy become dangerous?
4. Is the ending mostly sad, ironic, or both? Why?
5. Is Mr. Shap a tragic hero or a foolish madman?
5. Is there a message to this story? A critique?

## Grammatik: Modalverber

Modalverber er en undergruppe af hjælpeverber. På engelsk findes følgende fem modalverber. Hvert modalverbum har kun to forskellige former:

|  | can | shall | will | may | must |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Præsens** | can | shall | will | may | must |
| **Præteritum** | could | should | would | might | must |

Modalverbet er altid sat sammen med et verbum i infinitiv uden *to*.

> [!eksempel]
> - *Emma **can sing** quite beautifully.*
> - *Peter realized that he **could win** the race.*

## Paragraph of the week

### Walter

*Characterisation*
- Walter's fear controls his life so completely that he chooses isolation instead of freedom.

*Theme*
- Walter's vignette shows that trying to escape trauma can trap a person even more deeply.

*Setting*
- The dark, repetitive bunker setting ties together the concepts of fear and loneliness.

### The Coronation fo Mr. Thomas Shap

*Characterisation*
- Through his growing contempt for ordinary life, Mr. Shap is characterised as a man who would rather invent importance than accept his own powerlessness.

*Language*
- The story uses elevated and vivid language to make Mr. Shap’s fantasy world seem grand, beautiful, and seductive.

*Theme/message*
- The story presents fantasy not as harmless escape, but as a destructive withdrawal from reality.
