## Minute Cryptic

 ```minutecryptic
   clue: Child flies military aircraft, gaining ground on the slower pilots
   answer: maggots
   enumeration: 7
   definition: Child flies
   indicator: pilots
   fodder: military aircraft, gaining ground on the slower
   explanation: PILOTS indicates the first letters of the fodder -> MAGGOTS, which are CHILD FLIES
 ```

 ```minutecryptic
   clue: Stylish family saying "au revoir" to French friend
   answer: fly
   enumeration: 3
   definition: Stylish
   indicator: saying "au revoir" to | French
   fodder: family | friend
   explanation: FRENCH indicates FRIEND -> AMI. SAYING "AU RVOIR" indicates the FAMILY says goodbye to AMI -> FLY
 ```

## Discussion

- Why has Walter isolated himself? How has this afected his relationship with the world? 
- What is Walter afraid of? How has fear shaped his life?
- Looking at his postcards, is he the person he wants to be? What’s holding him back?
- What feelings do you think Walter was having just before he died? What is ironic about his death?

## PostSecret

- Look at the [PostSecret](https://postsecret.com) website. Discuss why this site exists and why people would send their secrets.
- What does this reveal about our private identities?
- What's the difference between this and social media?

## Writing

- Write a PostSecret for any two of the following: Molly, Barbara, Calvin, or Walter. Then, find an image that would be on the postcard and explain how it relates to the character/secret.
- Design a support system for this character. Would they call a hotline? Imagine a better route, the safety net and skill this character could have had.
- Why does PostSecret exist? Why do we need it at all? Would sending this postcard have helped? Can it backfire? Write a response.

## Paragraph of the week
*Characterisation*
- Walter's fear controls his life so completely that he chooses isolation instead of freedom.

*Theme*
- Walter's vignette shows that trying to escape trauma can trap a person even more deeply.

*Setting*
- The dark, repetitive bunker setting ties together the concepts of fear and loneliness.
