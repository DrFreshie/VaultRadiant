## Udvidet tid

Simpel tid anvendes til at beskrive:

- en kendsgerning
- en gentagen handling
- en permanent situation
- noget eviggyldigt.

> [!eksempel]
> - Peter plays football.
> - Peter goes to church every Sunday.
> - He lives in New York.
> - Two and two make four.

Udvidet tid bruges til at beskrive:

- en handling, der foregår her og nu
- parallelle handlingsforløb
- handlingsforløb, der danner baggrund for en afbrudt eller ny handling.

> [!eksempel]
> - Look! Peter is playing football.
> - Peter was standing in front of the house when Emma was driving by.
