1. Create 6 questions freely
2. Create 6 questions following Bloom's taxonomy

## Answering questions

- Answer questions in writing
    - Use as many examples and explanations as you can 
    - Optionally add association comments connected to the questions

## Discussion

- Look at the answers: Do you agree with them? Can you add something?

## Summary

1. Write a summary of What Remains of Edith Finch?
    - Present tense
    - 150 words 

## Paragraph

2. Write a paragraph explaining what you think is the main theme of the story
3. Write a paragraph explaining your own point about the story.
    - Use ACE-model
    - Try and make them *coherent*
