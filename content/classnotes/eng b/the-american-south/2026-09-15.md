## Warm-up: Podcast expectations

Discuss:

- What makes a podcast different from a written text?
- How can voice, music, pauses, and editing shape our opinion?
- When a journalist visits a small town, what responsibilities does he have?

## Introducing *S-Town*

*S-Town* begins as an investigative podcast about a possible crime in a small Alabama town. It quickly becomes a story about place, class, intelligence, loneliness, masculinity, and the ethics of telling other people's stories.

### Key words

- outsider narrator
- representation
- setting
- characterisation
- ethics
- Southern Gothic

## First impressions

1. How is Woodstock, Alabama introduced?
2. What impression do we get of John B. McLemore?
3. What impression do we get of Brian Reed?
4. Which sounds or voices stood out to you?
5. Does the podcast make the town seem real, strange, funny, tragic, or stereotypical?

## Character and setting notes

Create a short character profile of John B. McLemore.

Include:

- three adjectives
- two quotes or details from the podcast
- one contradiction in his personality
- one question you still have about him

## Exit-ticket

Answer in 2-3 sentences:

> Is *S-Town* more interested in solving a mystery or portraying a person and a place?
