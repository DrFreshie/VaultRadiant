## Warm-up: Recap in three quotes

Find three quotes from *The Kentucky Derby Is Decadent and Depraved*:

1. One quote that describes the place.
2. One quote that describes the people.
3. One quote that reveals the narrator's attitude. Explain why/how your chosen quote reveals the narrator's attitude.

## Gonzo analysis

### Discuss

1. What kind of narrator is Thompson?
2. Is he reliable? Why or why not?
3. What does he actually tell us about the Kentucky Derby?
4. What does he tell us about American society?
5. How are Southern people represented?
6. Does the essay criticise the South, America, rich people, crowds, journalism, or Thompson himself?

## Language

1. Where and why does Thompson describe going to a horse race as if he was going into war?
2. Characterise the language by finding examples of similes, metaphors, adjectives, colloquialisms.
    - *Example* "My eyes were swollen almost shut and the sudden burst of sunlight through the door left me stunned and helpless like a sick mole"

## Analysis model

Use ACE / PEE:

- *A/P* — Make a point about the text.
- *C/E* — Give a quote.
- *E/E* — Explain how the quote supports your point.

Example sentence starter:

> Thompson presents the Derby as a grotesque spectacle rather than a noble sporting event. This is clear when he writes "...". The quote suggests that...

## Writing: Thompson's South

Write one analytical paragraph answering:

> How does Hunter S. Thompson represent the American South in *The Kentucky Derby Is Decadent and Depraved*?

You must include:

- a clear point
- one quote
- an explanation
- the words *gonzo journalism* somewhere in your paragraph

## Exit-ticket

What is one advantage and one disadvantage of gonzo journalism?
