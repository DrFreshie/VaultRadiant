# West II - journey and violence

---
[[classnotes/eng b/the-american-south/index|the american south]]

## ⋮⋮ Program

- *08.35* Warm-up: Recap through objects
- *08.50* Reading: next section
- *09.20* Break
- *09.35* Violence and vulnerability
- *09.55* Language analysis
- *10.15* Quick-write
- *10.25* Exit-ticket

## Warm-up: Recap through objects

Choose one object, place, or image from the last reading.

Explain:

- where it appears
- what it tells us about a character
- what it might symbolise

## Reading: next section

Continue reading *West*.

Mark:

- moments of danger
- decisions that change the journey
- descriptions of bodies, weapons, animals, or landscape
- passages where the text feels beautiful, brutal, or strange

## Violence and vulnerability

Discuss:

1. Who has power in this section?
2. Who is vulnerable?
3. Is violence presented as heroic, ordinary, shocking, necessary, or meaningless?
4. How does the landscape affect the characters' choices?

## Language analysis

Choose one descriptive passage.

Annotate for:

- adjectives and verbs
- imagery
- sentence length
- mood
- contrast between beauty and danger

Then write:

> The language in this passage creates a mood of... because...

## Quick-write

Write for 8 minutes:

> In *West*, the journey reveals that America is...

Use at least one concrete detail from the text.

## Exit-ticket

What is one thing the journey has already changed?
