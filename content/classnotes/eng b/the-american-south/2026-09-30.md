## Warm-up: What has changed?

Choose one character from *West* and complete:

- At the beginning, this character...
- By the end, this character...
- The most important cause of this change is...

## Finish *West*

Finish reading *West*.

After reading, write down:

- one important image
- one important choice
- one unanswered question
- one sentence about what the ending means

## Ending discussion

1. Is the ending hopeful, tragic, ambiguous, or something else?
2. What has the journey achieved?
3. What has been lost?
4. How does the ending comment on American myths?
5. How does *West* connect to the American South unit even when it looks toward the West?

## Exam-style speaking preparation

Prepare a 2-minute mini-presentation:

> How does one text from the unit represent the American South or American myths?

Structure:

1. Brief summary
2. One analytical point
3. One quote or concrete example
4. Link to the unit theme

## Exit-ticket

Which text gave you the most complex view of the American South, and why?
