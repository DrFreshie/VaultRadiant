## More freedom in West

- *Level 1 -- Clarification:* What does Bellman leave behind when he goes west, and what does he take with him?
- *Level 2 -- Explanation:* Why does Bellman feel that he has to go even though Julie, Elmer, and other people think the journey is foolish?
- *Level 3 -- Analysis:* How is Bellman's freedom different from his sister Julie's?
- *Level 4 -- Interpretation:* Bellman chooses to go west, while Old Woman From A Distance's people are forced west. What does this contrast say about freedom in the novel?
- *Level 5 -- Perspective:* What is the cost of freedom in modern society and how does it compare with the context of the novel?

## Essay writing

1. Write an introduction to an analytical essay about freedom in the first 40 pages of *West* using the *funnel method*.
2. Make an outline for the structure of a full essay with first drafts of topic sentences for 3 paragraphs plus quotes to use in those paragraphs.
