## Warm-up: American myths

Discuss which myths about America appear in the unit:

- freedom
- success
- civilisation
- the frontier
- self-invention
- tradition
- the small town

Which of these myths does *West* support, and which does it challenge?

## Myth versus reality

### Group questions

1. What does the title *West* suggest at this point?
2. Is the journey still hopeful?
3. What does the text suggest about exploration and ambition?
4. Who pays the price for other people's dreams?
5. How does the text complicate the idea of America as a land of opportunity?

## Comparison with earlier texts

Choose one comparison:

### Option A: *West* and Thompson

- How do both texts challenge romantic ideas about America?
- How do humour, grotesque detail, or violence affect the reader?

### Option B: *West* and *S-Town*

- How do both texts present place as identity?
- How do outsiders misunderstand or interpret places?

### Option C: *West* and *A Rose for Emily*

- How do both texts show the past haunting the present?
- How do setting and decay shape the story?

## Exit-ticket

Write one analytical sentence using the word *myth*.
