## ⋮⋮ Program

- *08.35* Warm up
- *08.45* Reading circle 
- *09.30* Break

- *09.55* Lovecraft Country
- *10.55* Exit-ticket

## Warm up

- Hunter S. Thompson, *The Kentucky Derby Is Decadent and Depraved*
- Brian Reed, *S-Town*, Chapter 1
- Mark Twain, *The Adventures of Huckleberry Finn*, chapters 1-2
- Carys Davies, *West*

1. For each text, choose one keyword that connects it to the American South,
2. Chose the two texts that show the most different versions fo the south. Discuss how they connect.

## Reading circle

1. Discussion begins with the Bard
2. After the bard “sums up” the previous reading, they share their chosen passage
3. All other members share their chosen passage
4. Next, the Paladin shares their questions and discussion ensues
5. The Wizard shares words
6. The Ranger / Artificer shares
7. After assignments are shared, party members work on assignment of the day, and decide upon classes for the next reading assignment

## Assignment of the day

1. *Exam* Prepare speaking notes for a presentation of the first half of the novel focusing on characterisation. Present to a partner or record yourself presenting - your presentation should last appr. 8 minutes.
2. *Creative* Make a ...
    - Letter from Cy Bellman to his daughter
    - Translated transcript of Old Woman From a Distance speaking by the campfire one night.
    - Diary entry from Bess from years after the events in the book.

## Lovecraft Country

- Watch first episode of *Lovecraft Country*

## Exit-ticket

- Write for 5 minutes about the connection between the episode and at least one of the other texts.
