## Warm-up: Predictions

Before finishing the story, answer:

1. What do you think has happened?
2. Which details make you suspicious?
3. What role does the town play in the mystery?

## Finish reading

Finish *A Rose for Emily*.

After reading, write down:

- the most shocking detail
- one detail that foreshadowed the ending
- one question about Emily's motives
- one question about the town's responsibility

## Narrator and community

The narrator often says *we* instead of *I*.

Discuss:

- Who is *we*?
- What does this collective narrator know?
- What does it not know?
- Does the town help Emily, control Emily, gossip about Emily, or abandon Emily?
