## Warm-up: Who owns a story?

Discuss:

1. Is it okay to turn a real person's life into entertainment?
2. Does consent solve all ethical problems?
3. Are listeners responsible for how they judge real people in documentaries and podcasts?

## Listening: selected extracts

Listen to selected parts of *S-Town*.

Focus on:

- how John is characterised through his own voice
- how Brian Reed frames John
- how the town is described
- moments that feel intimate or private
- moments that feel like Southern Gothic

## Ethics discussion

### Corners

Choose a corner and prepare an argument:

1. *S-Town* is respectful and humanising.
2. *S-Town* is exploitative.
3. *S-Town* is both.
4. I am undecided.

Use examples from the podcast.

### Questions

- Does the podcast challenge stereotypes about the South or repeat them?
- How does Brian Reed's outsider position affect the story?
- Is John presented as a genius, a victim, an eccentric, or something else?
- What details should maybe have stayed private?
