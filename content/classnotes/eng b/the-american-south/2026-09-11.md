## Southern stereotypes and Southern Gothic

### Discussion

- What makes a setting feel "Southern"?
- What does the word *gothic* suggest?
- What are elements of a gothic story?

### Notes

Southern Gothic often includes:

- decaying settings
- grotesque or extreme characters
- family secrets
- social hypocrisy
- racism and historical guilt
- humour mixed with horror
- outsiders looking in%%
