## The Adventures of Huckleberry Finn

<div style="display:flex; gap:1.5rem; align-items:center;">
  <div style="width:55%;">
    <ul>
      <li>Look up 'The Adventures of Huckleberry Finn' and find a summary and/or a review. What is the novel about? Write three sentences with the most essential points on a piece of paper. Pass the paper to the person next to you and receive one from the person from the other side. Add a new sentence to this paper and pass it on. Remember to read the other person's notes so you don't write the same things. Let the papers pass 6-8 times and before you get your own paper back and read the summary.</li>

## Paragraph writing

Topic sentence: *The author uses humor by making a disparity between what the character understands and what the reader understands.*

1. What is Huck talking about in lines 37-53? What does he learn and how is it different from what the reader understands?
2. What is the effect of this?
3. Find three examples of humor or irony.
