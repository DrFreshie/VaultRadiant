## Formal vs. informal language

> [!infobox]
> > [!info] High/formal language
> > - Longer sentences.
> > - Correct grammar.
> > - Varied vocabulary.
> > - Use of subordinate clauses.
> > - Varied sentence structure.
> > - Few repetitions.
> > - Third person use.
> > - Neutral tone.
>
> > [!info] Low/informal language
> > - Short sentences.
> > - Mainly main clauses.
> > - Less varied vocabulary.
> > - Repetitions.
> > - Use of fillers.
> > - Alternate and incorrect spelling and grammar.
> > - Little variation in sentence structure.
> > - Contractions (such as I'll, you'd, ain't, won't).
> > - First person use.
> > - Personal tone (the use of we and I).

> [!task] In pairs or individually
