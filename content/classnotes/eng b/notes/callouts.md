# Callouts

## Standard callouts

   > [!note] Note
   > General notes and information.

   > [!abstract] Abstract
   > A summary of the main points.

   > [!info] Info
   > Useful information.

   > [!todo] To do
   > Something to complete.

   > [!tip] Tip
   > A helpful suggestion.

   > [!success] Success
   > Something completed successfully.

   > [!question] Question
   > Something to think about.

   > [!warning] Warning
   > Something requiring attention.

   > [!failure] Failure
   > Something that did not work.

   > [!danger] Danger
   > An important risk.

   > [!bug] Bug
   > A problem to investigate.

   > [!example] Example
   > A concrete illustration.

   > [!quote] Quote
   > Words worth remembering.

   ## Custom callouts

   > [!task] Discuss in pairs
   > 1. Read the text.
   > 2. Compare your answers.

   > [!task-columns]
   > > [!task] Option A
   > > 1. Write a short summary.
   >
   > > [!task] Option B
   > > 1. Create a mind map.

   > [!infobox]
   > > [!info] First box
   > > - First point.
   > > - Second point.
   >
   > > [!tip] Second box
   > > - Another point.
   > > - A useful suggestion.

   > [!box] hello
   > A neutral box without a heading.

   > [!neutral] hello2
   > An alternative name for the same neutral box.

   > [!pullquote]
   > The future depends on what we do in the present.
   >
   > <cite>Example attribution</cite>

   > [!no-slide] Website only
   > This appears on the page but is excluded from presenter mode.

   > [!definition] Definition
   > An even integer is an integer divisible by two.

   > [!bevis] Proof
   > Let n = 2k for some integer k. Then n is divisible by two.

   > [!eksempel] Eksempel
   > The integers 2, 4, and 6 are even.

   ## Collapsible callouts

   > [!question]- Initially closed
   > Click the heading to reveal this answer.

   > [!tip]+ Initially open
   > Click the heading to hide this content.
