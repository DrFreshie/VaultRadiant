# How to analyse..

(Always support your points with examples from the text)

## Fiction

### Setting

1. Describe the physical setting.
2. Analyse the effect: Does the setting create a certain atmosphere? Does this influence the story / characters?

### Narrator

1. Determine the type of narrator.
2. Analyse the effect: How does the narrator influence what we know about the characters and setting?

### Characterisation

1. Describe how the main character(s) are presented.
2. Analyse their inner life and role in the story: What do we know about their values, personality, and relationships with the other characters? Do they develop?

### Theme

1. Analyse the theme of the story: What does the story say about the theme? How is it visible in the story?

## Non-fiction

### Intention

1. Describe the sender, audience, circumstances, language and subject of the text (the rhetorical pentagram).
2. Describe the rhetorical situation of the text:
    - Determine the exigence: what change is the speaker hoping to achieve with the text?
    - Determine the constraints: What factors limit or influence the speaker's ability to persuade the audience?

### Method

1. Analyse the structure of the text: How does it support the intention?
2. Analyse the modes of appeal used in the text:
    - How credible is the writer/speaker, and how do they try to strengthen their credibility?
    - Does the writer/speaker try to make the audience be emotionally invested in the topic?
    - Does the writer/speaker try to convince the audience of something through logical arguments?
    - Are the modes of appeal used fitting for the context?
3. Describe the language of the text:
    - Are rhetorical devices used, and to what effect?
    - How is the tone (inclusive, formal, informal, serious, humorous, ironic, sarcastic, optimistic, pessimistic, sympathetic, critical)?

### Execution

1. Evaluate the effectiveness of the text:
    - How well does method serve intention?
    - How well does the text respond to the rhetorical situation?

## Film and television

1. Go through the steps in fiction or non-fiction above.
2. Analyse the use of cinematic techniques: How does it support e.g. characterisation, theme or intention?

## Graphic narratives

1. Go through the steps in fiction or non-fiction above.
2. Analyse the relationship between words and images: How do dialogue, captions, sound effects and images complement or contradict each other, and how does this support e.g. characterisation, theme, or intention?
