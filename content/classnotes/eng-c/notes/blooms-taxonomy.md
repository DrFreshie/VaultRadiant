# Bloom's taxonomy

| Level | What you do | Example question or task about *Severance* |
|---|---|---|
| **Remember** | Recall facts and identify details. | What happens when Helly wakes up at the table? |
| **Understand** | Explain meaning in your own words. | Explain how the severance procedure affects memory. |
| **Apply** | Use a concept or method in a particular situation. | Use the concept of private and public identity to explain Mark's two lives. |
| **Analyse** | Examine parts, patterns and relationships; explain how something works. | How do framing and lighting create a contrast between life inside and outside Lumon? |
| **Evaluate** | Make a judgement using evidence and clear criteria. | Does the procedure allow meaningful consent? Judge it using what the characters know and their ability to withdraw. |
| **Create** | Combine ideas into something new. | Write a scene in which an innie communicates with their outie, using details from the episode. |
