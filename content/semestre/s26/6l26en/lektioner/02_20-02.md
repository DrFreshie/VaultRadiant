## ≣ Tasks

### Voice

- Which idea of voice is mostly present in the texts we've read?
  - Is someone voicing an opinion about something?
  - Is someone given voice that normally doesn't have it?
  - Is the text somehow about how to express oneself?
  - Is it about the importance of having your own voice?

Come up with a line for each of the texts we've read.
-  What in each texts support your statement?

### Voice

- Make a mind map, either on paper or digitally, where each text is connected to different words connected to voice. Post your mind map on edaptio.

### Creative writing

- What do you find to be the most important aspect, or the one that interests you the most, about voice?

## %%Notes
So now we've tried to figure out the purpose of a non-fiction text. How can we extend that method to fiction texts? Is there even one purpose of a text - fiction or non-fiction? Or is it perhaps enough to say that 'this' is one voice of the text and this is how it shines through? Yes, with one caveat: you get points for saying something interesting about a text, and often what is interesting is inversely proportional to how obvious what you say is. SO... if you are asked to say something interesting about the texts we've read so far, and it has to be about voice in some way, what would you say? I can say that I've chosen these texts because I think that they - the texts - say something interesting about voice, or has an interesting voice. They are all 'about' many things, but at least one of the things that they are about is somehow connected to the concept of voice. 

- Which idea of voice is mostly present in the texts we've read? Is someone voicing an opinion about something? Is someone given voice that normally doesn't have it? Is the text somehow about how to express oneself? Is it about the importance of having your own voice? Come up with a line for each of the texts we've read. 
- What in each texts support your statement?
