## ⋮⋮ Program

- Belonging
- San Junipero
- Analysis and perspective

## ≣ Tasks

### Belonging

- What might make someone choose a virtual world over real life?
- Can a digital space feel more meaningful than the physical world?
- What themes do you expect from a story about memory, identity, love, ageing, and escapism?

### San Junipero

- While watching, note down moments where Yorkie or Kelly seem comfortable, uncertain, lonely, or connected.
- What kind of place is San Junipero? What does it offer people besides entertainment?

### San Junipero

- Make a short characterisation of Yorkie.
- Make a short characterisation of Kelly.
- What does each of them long for?
- In what ways do they belong in San Junipero, and in what ways do they not?

### San Junipero

- How does the episode use setting, music, costume, and lighting to create a feeling of nostalgia and freedom?
- Why is the contrast between San Junipero and the world outside it important?
- Is San Junipero presented as an escape, a second chance, or something more troubling?

### Belonging

- Does the episode suggest that belonging is something you find, something you choose, or something you build together with someone else?
- Does the ending feel hopeful, unsettling, or both? Why?
- What does the episode say more generally about loneliness, identity, and the need to be seen?

### Presentation

- Write 8-10 statements that could be used in an oral presentation about _San Junipero_ as a text about belonging.
- Include at least one point about characterisation, one about setting, and one about the ending.

## Notes

- _08.05_ Intro. Today we move into _Black Mirror_ and look at _San Junipero_ as a text about belonging.
- _08.10_ Short warm-up in pairs: what kinds of conflicts or questions might appear in a story about a virtual afterlife, memory, identity, love, ageing, and escapism?
- _08.20_ Sum up on the board. Collect keywords and likely themes from the episode: identity, memory, loneliness, freedom, technology, belonging, ageing, death, and second chances.
- _08.30_ Very short introduction to _Black Mirror_ and to the episode. While watching, note moments where Yorkie or Kelly seem alone, at ease, out of place, or deeply connected.
- _08.35_ Watch the first part of the episode.
- _09.05_ Short pause and sum up in pairs: what do we know now, and what still seems strange or unresolved?
- _09.15_ Break
- _09.25_ Watch the rest of the episode.
- _09.55_ Write for five minutes: what kind of belonging is each of the two main characters looking for?
- _10.05_ Break
- _10.15_ Group work with the analysis questions. Focus on characterisation, setting, the contrast between worlds, and the ending.
- _10.45_ Sum up together in class.
- _10.55_ Break
- _11.05_ Write 8-10 oral-presentation statements about the episode as a text about belonging.
- _11.20_ Final sum up: is the ending hopeful, unsettling, or both, and what does the episode finally suggest about belonging?
- _11.25_ Done
