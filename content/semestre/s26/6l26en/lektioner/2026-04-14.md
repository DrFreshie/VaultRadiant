# 14-04
---

## ⋮⋮ Program 
- Recorded conversations
- Reading time

## ≣ Tasks

### Recorded conversations
Listen to the beginning of the podcast:
 1. How is the work introduced
 2. What are the analytical questions being discussed?
 3. How are answers presented? Are turns taken, or is it more interview like?

### Recorded conversations
Start recording your answers to questions - record them one at a time or as one big conversation which you then have to edit afterwards. Try to mimic the parts you feel worked great in the podcast you listened to, and bring your own flavour to the mix if you want. You may want to consider having a host who asks the questions, and who can bring their opinions and examples into the discussion after the others have chimed in.

### Recorded conversations
You are allowed to
- Briefly discuss the questions before recording

You are not allowed to:
- Write manuscripts that you read out loud.
