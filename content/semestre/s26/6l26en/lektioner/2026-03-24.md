---

## ⋮⋮ Program 
- Weekend poems
- 'Out of Control' poem
- Grammatik:
  - Do som hjælpeverbum
  - Ordstilling

## ≣ Tasks

### Weekend poems
- Read your poem aloud to the others in your group, and discuss how the poem relates to the theme of belonging.
- Is there a rhyme scheme? A special rhythmic pattern?

### Weekend poems
- Read your poem aloud to the others in your group, and discuss how the poem relates to the theme of belonging.
- Read the Brian Patten poem out loud and listen to the sounds of the poem. Can you find:
  - a rhyme scheme
  - a rhythm
  - words you do not recognize
  - words you do not expect to find in a poem
  - words that are repeated
  - sentence structure: statements or questions? Simple or complex? Poetic or proselike?
- Which poem do you like best? Why?

### Out of Control
- Read the poem out loud, and discuss whether you like this poem or not, and why or why not.
- Discuss the questions you've prepared for this poem.

### Do-omskrivning.
  - "I might like it"
  - "I should like it"
  - "I like it"
  - "I do like it"
  - "Do I like it?"
