## %%Notes

### Plan

- First they find information about Christ church or UN, and write a speech about and give it to each other.
- Then we listen to the first five minutes of the speech, and quickly talk about whether they sense a difference.
- Then they read the rest of the speech and find four quotations that stands out to them, as extra impactful or just nice
- Then I talk about rhetorical tools
- Then they choose a section of about 25 lines and try to find examples of rhetorical tools
- Then we sum up and they present. in pairs, the four most awesome quotes, and explain why they chose them
- Then they look at their quotations and write a quick reflection on what they think makes a good speech; if it's not rhetorical tools, what then?
