# Opgave 5 - Omskriv med modalverber

Omskriv følgende sætninger, så betydningen i sætningen bevares. Erstat i hver sætning verbet med et modalverbum.

**Eksempel:** *It’s possible to find a matching tie.* → *You can find a matching tie.*

1. It’s likely that he is late. → He …
2. I’m sure she is Italian. → She …
3. You’re supposed to stay in bed. → You …
4. It was wrong of you to tell him the truth. → You …
5. They probably got on the wrong train. → They …
6. Peter wants to come next Friday. → Peter …
7. Peter has to quit drinking. → Peter …
8. It’s possible that he already left. → He …
9. I suggest cutting down on your expenses. → You …
10. Emma is able to come tomorrow. → Emma …
11. Emma is allowed to come tomorrow. → Emma …
