\subsection{}
\textbf{CREATIVE WRITING: MISSING PERSON REPORT}

Huckleberry Finn is mistreated by his father and decides to run away. Write a missing person report on Huck by using your analysis of the text. Write in the present tense.

Your missing person report should include the following points:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*., leftmargin=*]
  \item Basic information about Huck, such as name, date of birth or age, address, family, etc.
  \item Physical appearance and clothing: height, weight, build, particular marks, clothes, etc.
  \item Habits and personality. Consider the following questions:
  \begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\alph*.}, leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item Does Huck smoke or drink alcohol?
    \item Does Huck have any particular habits?
    \item What type of personality does Huck have? Is he outgoing or quiet? Friendly or depressed?
    \item What are Huck's personal values and philosophy?
    \item Is Huck religious?
    \item Does Huck have any personal or emotional problems?
    \item What level of education or training does Huck have?
  \end{enumerate}
  \item Huck's plans and/or activities on the day he goes missing.
  \item When Huck is last seen, and by whom.
  \item Huck's overall health and mental condition.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{}
\textbf{CREATIVE WRITING: WRITE YOUR OWN CHAPTER 3}

Write your own version of a third chapter to \textit{The Adventures of Huckleberry Finn}. Try to write in Huck's voice and use as many features of Southern American English (SoAE) as possible.

Before you write, make notes on these questions:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*., leftmargin=*]
  \item What happens after Huck runs away?
  \item Where does Huck go, and why does he choose this place?
  \item Who does Huck meet? Is this person helpful, dangerous, or both?
  \item What problem or conflict appears in the chapter?
  \item How can you show Huck's personality through his language, thoughts, and choices?
  \item Which SoAE elements can you include? Think about grammar, vocabulary, spelling, pronunciation, and sentence structure.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.5em}
\textbf{Checklist:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\alph*.}, leftmargin=1.5em]
  \item I write in the first person as Huck.
  \item I use the present or past tense consistently.
  \item I include details about setting, action, and Huck's feelings.
  \item I use several SoAE elements deliberately.
  \item I make the chapter sound like it belongs in the novel.
\end{enumerate}
