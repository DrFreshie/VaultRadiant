\subsection{}
\textbf{LANGUAGE: THE USE OF DIALECT}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*., leftmargin=*]
  \item In some parts of the novel, Twain has chosen to use a language that deviates from Standard American English and is grammatically incorrect. Look at the first paragraph and rewrite the sentences into Standard American English.

  \item Afterwards, answer the questions below.
  \begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\alph*.}, leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item What did you change? Consider vocabulary, grammar, and sentence structure.
    \item Why does Twain choose to use dialect and incorrect grammar?
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{}
\textbf{ANALYSIS: THE STRUCTURE OF THE NOVEL}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*., leftmargin=*]
  \item The novel starts with a \qm{Notice} and an \qm{Explanatory}. What is the author saying here?
  \item Why does he find it necessary to state these things?
  \item What is the function of the opening?
  \item What is the effect on the reader?
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{}
\textbf{ANALYSIS: THE NARRATOR AND HIS VOICE}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*., leftmargin=*]
  \item Who is the narrator of the text? Look at the first 5--6 lines of chapter 1 in particular.
  \item The narrator starts off by saying \qm{YOU don't know\ldots}. Who is the \qm{you}?
  \item The narrator also mentions Mark Twain in the opening. What is the effect of this?
\end{enumerate}
